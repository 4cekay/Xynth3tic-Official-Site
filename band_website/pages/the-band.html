About Us

Have you ever considered what happens to that which is deemed unnecessary? When something is cast aside and stripped of its meaning. Denied purpose. Broken, Discarded, Lost. To most, these things never come to mind again. But in the dark of the night, if you know where to look, you may find them gathered under flickering candlelight, forging a purpose of their own.

A trio of gothic mavericks, looking to pave a path towards a world of their own, where diversity is welcomed and embraced.
Consisting of Leader, Composer, and Lyricist Xynth3tic, Guitarist/Bassist M4ze, and Keyboardist Rend1ng, this triad invites you to waltz through the open door to set free the words left unsaid.

Xynth3tic

"Synthetic" (adj.)

Not natural. False or Artificial. This word has been used to describe people like her. To make them seem fake. Less human. But that's a lie.

"Everyone learns to act a certain way. To put on a show. What makes me any different?"
And so she takes this name as her own. Because in a way, isn't everyone [Xynth3tic]?

The Leading Lady.
With her expressively melancholic compositions and ethereal vocals, she's the main driving force behind the band's creative voice.

M4ze

"Maze" (n.)

Path after path, passage after passage. Walls designed to lead the aimless wanderers toward dead end after dead end. Why do the foolish persist? They proceed, turning corner after corner, blinded by all the different colours of the beacon. Why is it only she, who cannot see its light?

"You know, it's impolite to request a lady doff her mask. Not with that lost gaze of yours, and certainly not without the promise that you can handle what lies beneath."

The band's Bassist.
Responsible not only for supporting the music with her string mastery, but also for shaping the band's public presence. She designed the website you're viewing now.

Rend1ng

"Rending" (v.)

To tear apart, to sever. A present neatly wrapped with ribbons, one that can only unravel with force. Treat with cruelty, and rip unkindly; it is the only form of existence they understand.

"Your softness is a weapon, your pity defiling. Admire only from a distance, lest you understand hurt as care."

The band's Pianist/Keyboardist, as well as the concept artist & graphic designer.
They are responsible for taking the ideas of their fellow bandmates and giving them form, fostering the band's eerie image.
